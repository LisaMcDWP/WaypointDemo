This is a test of the Bland webchat.

Adding a Bland webchat to your organization’s website allows patients, members, or providers to interact with your virtual assistant in real time through a simple chat window. The webchat embeds directly on your site with just a few lines of script, branded with your organization’s logo, colors, and header. It provides the same conversational pathways you’ve built for phone or SMS outreach, but in a familiar, text-based interface that feels like live chat support. This makes it easy to engage visitors, capture structured data, and guide them to the right next steps without requiring a phone call.
